Who we are · 01

In Sanskrit, līlā is divine play: the world created for the joy of it.

Our symbol is a putto riding two snakes. One serpent is fear, the other curiosity. Creation happens in between.

Watch children at play. When they pretend to be someone else, they are completely serious, or the game falls apart. We work the same way: curious, open, joyful, and committed to every detail.

Too much fear kills ideas. Too much curiosity without control creates chaos. We ride both, seriously, never solemnly.
